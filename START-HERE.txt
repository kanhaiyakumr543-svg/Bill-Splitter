DEPLOY IN 3 STEPS (no AI key or bill-reading API required)

1. Put this whole folder on GitHub.
2. On render.com, create a new Blueprint from the repository and deploy.
3. Open the Render URL on your phone or computer.

Bill photos are read by free OCR in the browser and are not uploaded to the server.
The host may sleep when idle; free-host limits and availability can change.
